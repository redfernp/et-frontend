How Paddy Power acca insurance works

Every version of the offer we have seen works the same basic way:
  1. Opt in, if required, and place a pre-match football accumulator that meets the minimum number of legs.
  2. Every leg must be at or above the minimum odds.
  3. If exactly one leg loses, Paddy Power refunds your stake as a free bet, up to a daily maximum.
  4. The free bet is credited the next day and expires after a set number of days.
The refund is a free bet, not cash, so the free bet stake isn't returned with any winnings.

Terms that have varied

These are the terms that differ between versions of the offer. Check each one before you bet:
Minimum legs 4+ in some versions, 5+ in others
Minimum odds per leg Usually 1/5
Maximum refund £10 or £20 a day in different versions
Bet Builders / Same Game Multis Included in some versions, excluded in others
Eligible leagues Usually UK and top European leagues, Champions League, Europa League and senior internationals

Things that usually don't qualify

  • Bets placed in-play
  • Bets that are cashed out
  • Multiple bets such as a Lucky 15 or Yankee (straight accas only)
  • Accas where a postponed or abandoned match takes you below the minimum number of legs
