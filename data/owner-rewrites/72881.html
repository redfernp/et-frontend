How to claim it

  1. Open a new QuinnBet account through our link and make a deposit.
  2. On your first day, place at least 3 bets on different events, including at least one bet of £10 or more at odds of evens (2.00) or bigger.
  3. Two of your bets must each be at least half the size of your largest stake. For example, if your largest bet is £10, two of your other bets need to be £5 or more.
  4. QuinnBet adds up your profit or loss from your first settled bet until 23:59 that day.
  5. The free bet is credited the day after your bets settle.

Worked example

You place three bets on your first day: £10, £5 and £5, all at evens or bigger, on different events. By 23:59 you are £20 down. QuinnBet refunds 50% of that loss, so you get a £10 free bet. If you had finished the day in profit, or lost less than £10, you would still get the guaranteed £5 free bet, because you placed a £10 bet at evens or bigger.

Using the free bet

  • Use it within 7 days, on odds between 1/4 (1.25) and 250/1.
  • It can be used on singles and straight accumulators (doubles, trebles and so on), but not on multiple bets such as a Trixie, Patent or Yankee.
  • The free bet stake is not returned with any winnings.
  • It must be used as one bet and can't be cashed out.

Bets that don't count

  • Void bets
  • Bets settled at SP
  • Bets placed with free bets
  • Cashed out bets
