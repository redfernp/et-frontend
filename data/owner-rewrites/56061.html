Last updated: 4 October 2026 Pick Your Punt is Betfred's name for building your own bet on a single football match. Instead of choosing from ready-made markets, you combine the outcomes you want, such as a team to win, a player to score and over a set number of corners, into one bet with one price. Betfred's own match previews now mostly call this a Bet Builder, but the Pick Your Punt name is still used on its promotions pages. This guide explains how it works and what to check before you use it. [fl_builder_insert_layout id=57231]

How Pick Your Punt works

  1. Open a football match on the Betfred website or app.
  2. Choose the Bet Builder (Pick Your Punt) option for that match.
  3. Add the selections you want from the available markets, such as match result, goalscorers, cards, corners and shots.
  4. Betfred prices the whole combination as a single bet. Check the price and stake, then place it.
All your selections have to win for the bet to pay out. Because the outcomes come from the same match, Betfred prices them together, so the combined odds are usually shorter than multiplying the individual prices.

Request a bet

Pick Your Punt began as a request-a-bet service: punters tweeted the bet they wanted with the #PickYourPunt hashtag and Betfred replied with a price. If you can't find a combination in the app, it is worth checking whether Betfred is currently taking requests on social media, but don't rely on it. The in-app builder is the main way to place these bets now.

Things to check before you bet

  • Which matches are covered: the builder is only available on selected leagues and matches.
  • Settlement rules: each market has its own rules, for example what counts as a card or a shot. A void selection can change how the bet settles.
  • Cash out: not every builder bet can be cashed out.
  • Maximum payout: builder bets can have their own maximum payout, which is lower than for standard bets. Check it before placing a big-priced bet.
  • Other promotions: offers such as Double Delight and Hat-trick Heaven apply to specific markets and may not apply to builder bets.
