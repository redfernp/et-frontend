A passing staging menu build is not evidence that Eric's future live menu edits will reach Astro.
2. **Exact paths and SEO:** build a production-mode route set, then fail closed on missing known live-200 routes, preview banners/meta, accidental noindex, wrong canonical, dropped source meta description/title, staging hostname, and redirect collisions. The source 4 October build reported hundreds of intentional robots/canonical mismatches. Keep the PPC Bet365 variant and paginated archives distinct. Use a fresh live URL/redirect inventory because the September crawl is dated.
3. **Template/data parity:** compare the priority routes side by side at desktop and mobile, including sidebar, orange/blue CTA hierarchy, author boxes, tables/accordion, footer and 18+/safer-gambling disclosures. No current Ninja-table feed means no replacement tips page.
4. **Operational rollback:** deploy first to a separate noindex review hostname, test a rendered crawl and navigation/404/301s, record a rollback to the existing WordPress origin, and only then decide on a public route switch. Never treat a passing preview-safety audit as a public launch gate.

That is evidence the preview remains safely inert, not evidence of readiness.
