For each canonical offer record: store a stable ID, bookmaker relationship, offer type, exact title, displayed approved space-restricted terms, source URL, country eligibility, status, verification person/date, and destination restricted from public GraphQL. Separately verified full terms or an official terms URL may be stored optionally. The same record should feed the homepage ladder, free-bets page, reviews, sidebars and in-article placements. Existing pages are **source material**, not seven independently maintained offers.

USPs are **optional bookmaker-level editorial fields**, not part of the offer terms: up to three short, reviewable reasons to consider a bookmaker. A separate optional offer highlight may describe a particular promotion. Neither may replace eligibility or terms, and a missing USP should simply hide that UI element. The seven displayed lines above mix enduring bookmaker strengths with promotion-specific or time-sensitive claims, so migrate them initially into the **offer/card highlight** field, not automatically into permanent bookmaker USPs. For example, BetWright's Bangers N' Cash line describes that rewards programme, while 10Bet's redesign claim can go stale. Bookmaker-level USPs can be curated separately later. Do not create seven welcome offers by default: BetWright's rewards programme and any future existing-customer promotions need their own offer types.

Do not publish from this inventory alone. Draft import and the GraphQL/Astro rendering test come first; revalidation and explicit placement approval follow. In particular, the seven-day validity of a credited free bet is not the offer's end date.
